The public name is living in a cat world, written in lowercase. The robot cat head identifies the project on the masthead and browser tab.

Color and type#

Warm ivory (#faf9f5) is the page background. Dark petrol (#172a29) carries the text. Teal (#21665b) identifies links, selections and the second line of the main title. Sage-gray rules (#d4dacf) divide sections. Hardware drawings use ochre for fasteners.

Space Grotesk is the display face. Descriptions use the system sans serif. Dimensions and edition labels use monospace. The Space Grotesk license accompanies the font.

Showing the machine#

Use drawings exported from the mechanical model. Keep the same orientation when comparing editions and label measurements with their reference surfaces. The V1 drawing shows its open-frame mechanism. V2 illustrations show the proposed console at its nominal camera height. Describe a physical result when someone has measured or observed it on the machine.

Navigation and language#

The overview links to the prototype and the console. Main pages are available in English and Spanish. The V1 workbench links to its Spanish illustrated assembly manual. The V2 viewer opens the model and its six inspection views.
