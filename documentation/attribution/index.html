Maintaining the ledgerContributingAttribution and licensesSource and development

The mechanical foundation derives from PhysiClaw, hardware v0.51, commit 8f610d48642aef7d974d3ce09d28e14a4a99bbae, by Qiao Qian, under MIT. Its original notice is retained in hardware/ft412/LICENSE.

The blog code and our original adaptations are published under MIT. Original text and data are also offered under MIT with attribution. Third-party names and trademarks belong to their owners.

Earlier planning reference photographs and manufacturers’ STEP files are not redistributed here. Product links point to their original sources. The public release contains 16 final parts in STL and STEP, three coupons and CAD review records; it is not the complete commercial-reference archive.

Complete regeneration scripts will be documented in a later release. STEP files allow geometry editing, but this initial repository does not promise one-command parametric regeneration of the full assembly.

The prototype has not been printed, assembled or physically tested. Validation of our modifications is not attributed to the original project.

The visual identity includes an original AI-generated robot cat mark and conceptual hardware illustration. The self-hosted Space Grotesk font is licensed under SIL OFL 1.1; its notice is retained in public/assets/fonts/OFL-SpaceGrotesk.txt.
