The mechanical foundation of V1 comes from PhysiClaw hardware v0.51 by Qiao Qian, commit 8f610d48642aef7d974d3ce09d28e14a4a99bbae. The upstream work is distributed under MIT. Its original copyright and permission notice accompanies the derivative hardware in hardware/ft412/LICENSE. PhysiClaw at the referenced commit · Original MIT notice

For the FT412 V1 adaptation, the project contributors adapted the mechanics to FramingTech slot-5 profiles, printed the motor mounts, and developed the touch head for the Adafruit 412. The later cradle and tip-coupler REV01 files address the belt-clamp interference and the measured size of the mesh tip. Assembly illustrations and prototype test observations are documented by this project. V1 hardware guide · Release scope

For V2, the project contributors designed the console enclosure, rail supports, electronics mounts, optical bridge and fastener layout around the retained components. The console geometry and the recorded clearance checks belong to this project's redesign. Manufacturers retain their names and trademarks. The equipment shapes in the console model are dimensional references. V2 design
