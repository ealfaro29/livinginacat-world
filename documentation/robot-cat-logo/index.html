Design and assets

Visual identityHardware illustrationRobot cat logo

Mode: built-in image generation. Original transparent PNG: public/assets/brand/robot-cat-v1.png. The 180 px and 48 px copies preserve transparency and serve the site header and favicon.

Use case: logo-design. Create ONE simple robot cat head logo for a personal open-hardware blog named "living in a cat world". No text, letters or wordmark. New original icon, not based on a photo or any existing character.
A compact front-facing mechanical cat head: two unmistakable pointed ears integrated into a softly squared faceplate, one broad dark visor with two small warm-white rectangular eyes, a tiny red nose, at most two short whisker marks per side. Strong memorable silhouette, balanced near symmetry, thick clean contours, subtle handmade imperfection. Only warm off-white #f3eee5, charcoal #141414 and vermilion red #ed3c36. Flat graphic mark with no shading, gradients, lighting, bevels, texture, chrome render or 3D. Warm off-white outer silhouette, red ear interiors and small mechanical details, dark visor. Must read clearly as both a cat and a robot at 32 px on near-black #111112. Centered head fills most of a square with a small transparent margin. Transparent background; no mockup, scene, framing circle, sticker border, grid, drop shadow or extra objects.
