# Ledger maintenance

Public source: `data/ledger.json`. USD amounts are integer cents. `null` means unknown. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD. `updatedAt` is the review date, not a live query timestamp.

## Overlapping records: do not add together

- `items`: original equipment list and quotes. Quantity means purchase units, not always individual parts. Quoted total = quantity × quoted unit price.
- An unquoted local purchase keeps both quote amounts `null`; downloads leave numeric price cells blank. The known equipment estimate excludes it and states how many lines remain unquoted. Stable item IDs survive a replacement or vendor change; `previousProcurement` retains the earlier quote. A cancelled vendor's quote remains historical even after the current items move to another vendor.
- `equipmentAllowances`: owner-requested planning amounts, kept separate from item quotes, orders and verified payments. The equipment estimate is item quotes plus these allowances.
- `orders`: vendor-level quote, documentary order total and verified payment where evidence exists. `invoiceTotalCents` is the order amount; `shippingInvoicedCents`, `taxInvoicedCents` and `tariffInvoicedCents` describe its charges. An order confirmation alone does not establish payment. The owner may confirm shipped purchases are paid; record this as owner confirmation rather than bank verification. `actualPaidCents` and the `*PaidCents` fields remain unknown when a reliable amount is unavailable. For a partially shipped order, do not assume the whole order is paid or invent an allocation. For a vendor aggregate, `paymentPartial` marks a subtotal across confirmed paid orders. Paid tax and shipping are breakdowns of the paid total, not additional amounts to add again.
- An `awaiting_invoice` order acknowledges a request awaiting the vendor's final invoice. `invoiceTotalFinal: false` and `shippingChargeFinal: false` distinguish provisional amounts from final charges; neither establishes payment or dispatch.
- `amazonOrders`: anonymized extracts of confirmed orders. They include equipment and extras. Documentary order totals are distinct from reconciled bank payments. When complete carrier fulfillment is verified, the owner's existing confirmation that shipped purchases are paid can cover the documented total; retain the owner-confirmation source and the bank-settlement caveat.
- `otherExpenses`: domain and grouped **Taxes, tariffs, shipping and others** totals per vendor. `includedInVendorOrderTotals: true` prevents double counting. The grouped total includes documented vendor shipping, taxes, tariffs and other included costs. Keep the underlying actual tax and tariff evidence in the private records; do not describe other purchases as tax. Speedy charges in CRC are separate. A standalone owner-confirmed project expense uses the same grouped label with `includedInVendorOrderTotals: false`; its verified payment is added once to project totals. It creates no equipment, vendor order or shipment record when the owner confirms there is no shipping.
- `forwardingCharges`: one dated combined Speedy amount in CRC for **all current packages**, with no filter by project membership. Review the entire current account and all current consolidations, including packages not yet quoted. Review active `Recibido`, `En Costa Rica` and `Enviado` queues without filtering charges by a known consolidation, project match or earlier review. `Enviado` alone does not prove payment. Exclude records confirmed `Pagado` or `Entregado` unless newer evidence establishes an outstanding charge. When a new consolidated parcel coexists with its original records and their billing replacement relationship is not verified, use `hasUnresolvedConsolidationCharges` in `aggregateForwardingCharges`. It returns `amountCRC: null` and `quoteStatus: reconciliation_pending`; publish the pending status without a numeric total. Preserve the last verified amount privately and in dated history. Do not infer charge replacement from similar weights, dates or counters. Use `aggregateForwardingCharges` to sum every quoted current package once; unquoted packages keep `quoteStatus: awaiting_remaining_quotes` and must never be treated as free or omitted from the review. Publish only the combined amount, currency, observation date, coverage and payment/finality caveats. Package identities, counts, weights, individual charges, matching evidence and the complete observation history stay outside user-facing views. The owner explicitly requires this full scope. Do not add restricted package details to project records or location counts. Do not mark the amount paid or combine it with USD totals without evidence, and do not assume it is the final consolidated balance.
- `shipments`: real project shipment or preparation record. `trackingAssigned` records that the private project shipment has a verified assigned number; the number itself stays private. It does not establish carrier acceptance, Speedy receipt or recipient delivery. `status` describes the carrier; `speedyStatus` describes separately confirmed forwarder progress. `speedyLocation` is `miami` or `costa_rica` only when Speedy's exact tracking entry supports it; otherwise it is `null`. `speedyCheckedAt` records the review date, and `speedyConfirmedAt` records the latest affirmative observation. Planned dispatch is distinct from estimated arrival. Complete `deliveredAt` only with recipient-delivery evidence. A suffix-only tracking match is insufficient for a project location assertion.
- `funding`: preserve unknown amounts until reconciled. Do not publish individual supporters or contributions without authorization.

Carrier-verified tracking aliases may establish project identity when Speedy scans a longer carrier barcode. Keep both exact references and the independent carrier evidence privately. Matching a suffix alone is insufficient. Cancelled orders retain their original documentary amounts and grouped charges, with unknown payments and refunds unchanged. Exclude them from active order totals, active grouped-cost summaries and the tracking queue. Cancellation does not itself establish a refund. Confirmed replacements get their own order and preparation record; a planned local purchase gets no imported shipment or Speedy prealert.

Keep cancelled shipment records in `shipments` as history. `activeShipments` excludes them, and shipments whose parent order is cancelled, from public tracking groups, coverage and location counts. A vendor-reported refund may have `refundCents` while `refundStatus: vendor_reported` and `refundBankSettlementConfirmed: false` preserve the distinction from a verified bank credit.

Owner-confirmed payment and recipient delivery exclude historical forwarding charges even when Speedy retains an old `Enviado` label. Pass explicitly settled references through `aggregateForwardingCharges`'s `settledTrackings` option; preserve those references and the confirmation privately. Do not exclude an unsettled charge merely because it is old or marked `Enviado`. Only new evidence of an outstanding balance can reopen a confirmed settlement.

## Public and private boundary

Never mention or display personal purchase details in messages, the public site, downloads or the interactive owner room. Preserve operational evidence outside those views; shared-order originals are not downloadable through the owner room. Public records retain their exact grouped amounts under Taxes, tariffs, shipping and others without product names, product identifiers, quantities, photos, invoices or descriptions that reveal those purchases. Preserve actual tax and tariff amounts; do not relabel other purchases as tax. Shipment updates for this category use a generic title. Never copy private admin data or original invoice contents into public source, posts, downloads, screenshots or commit messages. `npm run check` blocks restricted identities before publication.
