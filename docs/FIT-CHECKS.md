# First PLA Silk fit checks

Reported by the owner on November 13, 2026. The first test print batch was separately observed complete. These observations concern initial coupons, not acceptance of the complete robot.

| Check | Reported result |
|---|---|
| M3 | Passed, according to the owner |
| M5 | Passed, according to the owner |
| M4 | Not confirmed |
| Coupler fit | Not confirmed |

The test material was PLA Silk with a 0.4 mm nozzle. The owner did not itemize results for each round hole, square-nut pocket or countersunk seat, and no measured clearance diameters were supplied. The report therefore does not establish a chosen compensation value or validate every individual feature.

On November 14 the owner confirmed that the second batch — one phone bed and four feet — finished with a good print outcome. The exact completion time and measured fit were not supplied. The owner also selected PLA Silk for the final parts. This is the current material choice; it does not establish strength, creep or thermal performance.

The third plate is prepared and sliced: one motor mount A, one motor mount B and one belt clamp. It uses the main 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, six walls, six top/bottom layers and 80% gyroid infill. Bambu Studio estimates 2 h 51 min and 47.48 g. The job has not been sent to print.

M4 and coupler fit, complete assembly acceptance, motion, touch accuracy and thermal/load performance remain unvalidated. The earlier PETG settings remain historical reference material; PETG itself has not been tested here. The choice of PLA Silk does not change the CAD geometry or complete functional acceptance.
