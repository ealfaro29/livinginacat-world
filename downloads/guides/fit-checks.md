# First PLA Silk fit checks

M3/M5 reported by the owner on November 13, 2026; M4 fit reported on November 15. The first test print batch was separately observed complete. These observations concern initial coupons, not acceptance of the complete robot.

| Check | Reported result |
|---|---|
| M3 | Passed, according to the owner |
| M5 | Passed, according to the owner |
| M4 | Owner reports actual screws and nuts fit on November 15 |
| Coupler fit | Not confirmed |

The test material was PLA Silk with a 0.4 mm nozzle. The owner did not itemize results for each round hole, square-nut pocket or countersunk seat, and no measured clearance diameters were supplied. The report therefore does not establish a chosen compensation value or validate every individual feature.

On November 14 the owner confirmed that the second batch — one phone bed and four feet — finished with a good print outcome. The exact completion time and measured fit were not supplied. The owner also selected PLA Silk for the final parts. This is the current material choice; it does not establish strength, creep or thermal performance.

On November 14 (Costa Rica), the owner confirms the motor-mount A, motor-mount B and belt-clamp batch is printed. Bambu Studio separately shows the named job Finished at 100%, 299/299 layers. Exact completion time and measured fit were not supplied. The earlier 90-layer preparation and its estimates remain historical; they are not the actual finished job record.

The fourth plate contains one solenoid cradle and two clamp bars, in PLA Silk with the main 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, six walls, six top/bottom layers and 80% gyroid. It was observed starting with Homing toolhead at 0/145 layers. Slicer estimates are 1 h 10 min and 16.35 g; completion and actual consumption remain unconfirmed. Its complete plate overview was reviewed; an individual first-layer/cavity-roof inspection was not completed before the observed send.

On November 15 (Costa Rica), a fifth plate is prepared and sliced: one original tube holder and one Adafruit 5648 retainer. It uses PLA Silk, the main 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, six walls, six top/bottom layers, 80% gyroid and an 8 mm outer brim. Removable supports are enabled and present in the slice. The final estimates are 52 min 53 s and 6.32 g, not measured duration or consumption. The files have not been sent to the printer. The fourth batch still has no confirmed completion or measured-fit result.

On November 15 the owner confirmed that the actual M4 screws and nuts fit. No measured clearances or individual hole, pocket or seat results were supplied. At the owner's request, the fifth plate was expanded to eight pieces: the tube holder, Adafruit 5648 retainer, two front idler mounts, two motor-side idler mounts and the left/right XY joints. Original geometries, scale and manifest quantities are verified. The prior two-piece preparation is preserved. The revised plate keeps PLA Silk, main 0.4 mm, 0.20 mm nominal layers, six walls, six top/bottom layers, 80% gyroid, an 8 mm brim and removable tree supports. Its exported slice estimates 4 h 8 min 20 s, 64.17 g and 21.51 m. These are estimates; the revised plate has not been sent to print. The large electronics holder remains pending S301T clearance verification.

Coupler fit, complete assembly acceptance, motion, touch accuracy and thermal/load performance remain unvalidated. The earlier PETG settings remain historical reference material; PETG itself has not been tested here. The choice of PLA Silk does not change the CAD geometry or complete functional acceptance.
