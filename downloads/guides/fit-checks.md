On November 17, the reported inability to seat the original L-shaped cradle was reproduced against the authentic belt-clamp STEP. Its two ribs intersect the clamp even in the correct six-hole mounting orientation. [Cradle REV01](/documentation/cradle-rev01/) moves each rib 3 mm inward without changing the six mounting holes, four nut cavities or exterior size. Digital interference checks with the clamp, conservative carriage-screw-head envelopes and belt-channel envelopes pass; the STL is watertight and connected. **The replacement has not been sliced, printed or physically fitted.** The original cradle installation is blocked; the prior completed-print record is preserved. No printer action was performed.

The owner subsequently reports the solenoid installed with both bars and four screws. This does not identify the printed cradle revision or establish measured return, clearance or load performance.
