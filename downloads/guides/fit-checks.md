# First PLA Silk fit checks

Reported by the owner on November 13, 2026. The first test print batch was separately observed complete. These observations concern initial coupons, not acceptance of the complete robot.

| Check | Reported result |
|---|---|
| M3 | Passed, according to the owner |
| M5 | Passed, according to the owner |
| M4 | Not confirmed |
| Coupler fit | Not confirmed |

The test material was PLA Silk with a 0.4 mm nozzle. The owner did not itemize results for each round hole, square-nut pocket or countersunk seat, and no measured clearance diameters were supplied. The report therefore does not establish a chosen compensation value or validate every individual feature.

PETG fit, complete assembly acceptance, motion, touch accuracy and thermal performance remain unvalidated. The PLA Silk checks do not replace tests in the intended final material. A second batch was started, but its completion has not been confirmed in this record.

The published CAD archive remains the original v0.1 snapshot; its files, quantities and checksum have not changed.
