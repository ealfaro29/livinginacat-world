## How the record will continue

When packages arrive, we will compare items, quantities and physical condition with the order. The ledger will distinguish estimated dates from receipt dates and record problems, substitutions or returns if they occur. Data can be downloaded as CSV, JSON and Markdown.

Original emails and invoices stay private because they contain personal information. The site publishes amounts, source types and relevant status. GitHub history will preserve corrections and show how the information changed.

The next material milestone is resolving orders that are not progressing and receiving the parts. The next technical milestone is printing fit coupons and proving the fit with real components. Both will be documented here.
